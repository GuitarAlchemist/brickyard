# Delta's manual

## Welcome

Delta is a small robot you talk to aloud, in English or in French. It stands in a brick hall and is Claude Code's voice: it hears your microphone, passes what you say to your session, and Claude answers through its mouth.

This manual is a book on the hall's lectern (key 9). The chapters are on the left: a click opens one, and the arrows at the bottom turn the pages.

What you say is transcribed on your computer. Only the text goes to Claude, as what you type does.

## Talking to Delta

- Start your sentences with "Delta, …", or with "Claude, …" to talk to its brain.
- The conversation then goes on without the name: each sentence and each answer extend it by 30 seconds, which a ring counts down at the bottom left of the window.
- A sentence it lets pass for lack of "Delta" shows for five seconds under the robot, with how to call it: you know it heard you.
- It ignores a sentence that says "I'm not talking to you", or that first calls another session: Brutal, Gaia, Indigo, Amber, Codex or IX.
- It obeys a few short sentences by itself: "Delta, move to the top left", "maximize your window", "Delta, show me the sessions".

In Claude Code there are two ways to talk to it: restart Claude Code with Delta's channel, or run /delta:talk in any session, until "goodbye".

## The window's buttons

- The language (FR, FR·EN, EN), or the L key. With two languages, Delta hears both and answers each sentence in its own language. You can also say it: "speak English only", or in French « passe en mode bilingue ».
- The microphone mutes the computer's microphone, struck through in red, then turns it back on. A paired phone still talks to it.
- The phone, or the P key, shows the QR code that pairs a phone when the gateway has just made one. The code lives two minutes.
- Invite shows a QR code and a link that let guests watch the window.
- The M key opens the shelf: Claude Code sessions, plugins and marketplaces.

## Moving about

### Walking

- WASD, ZQSD or the arrows: move. Shift: sprint. Space: jump. C or Ctrl: crouch, and when sprinting slide.
- Q and E (on AZERTY: A and E): lean. Caps Lock: walk slowly.
- Right button held, or Ctrl then a click: look around.

### Flying

- F switches between walking and flying. Flying, Space or E rises, C descends, Shift goes faster. The wheel sets the pace.

### The views

- 1: the drift round Delta. Home or Enter: back to Delta.
- 2 the agents' office, 3 Gaia, 4 the whole hall, 5 the clocks, 6 the arcade cabinet, 7 the knots' floor, 8 the gallery, 9 the lectern.
- Shift + 1 to 5: the filmed view. 0: the cinematic, which any key or click ends.
- Tab: the tool wheel. F1: the keys' card.

## The agents' office

Key 2. Each Claude Code session has its station: a desk and a small Delta. The stations make a round tower: a wmux workspace's sign, then its robots. When a ring is full, the tower grows a storey. The rings turn slowly.

- The robot types while its session works, lights a red beacon while it waits for an answer, and jumps, arms up, when it has finished.
- A stopped robot bows its head. Amber: its session no longer runs. Grey: nothing is known of it.
- A click selects a robot: a small light turns above it and its ring holds still. A double click opens its pane in wmux.

## The hall

- Gaia (key 3): her placard says how the bridge to Gaia stands.
- The clocks of Ottawa and Paris (key 5). H hides or shows them.
- The arcade cabinet (key 6): a click on it starts PARSEC. Every key is then the game's, and Esc leaves it.
- The knots' floor (key 7): 21 sailors' knots in rope, at the top of the stair.
- IX's workshop: the knot IX describes, on its stand, and its origami table.

## The gallery and the lectern

Key 8. The left wall holds the learn site's courses, demos and artifacts. At the back, Streeling University's card catalogue has a drawer per department.

- A click on a book or a drawer opens it here, on the lectern. A click on a demo or an artifact opens its page in the browser.
- On the left, the outline: a click reads a lesson. On the right, the page, and the arrows turn it.
- "Open on the site" opens the page in the browser. "Delta's manual", at the bottom left, comes back to this book.
- The pages come from spareilleux.github.io/learn only. They are kept for a day, and read offline.

## Picture and performance

- B: the haze. T: the room's captions.
- F6: max out. F7: DLSS on the normal picture (the hall drawn smaller, then upscaled, its edges smoothed). F9: path tracing. F10: the RTX card.

## Hand gestures

Delta sees a few gestures when you decide: uv run hands\delta_hands.py turns the camera on until Ctrl+C.

- A thumbs up makes it smile.
- The "I love you" sign sends hearts.
- A wave opens the conversation.

The pictures stay in memory on your computer: only the gesture's name goes to Delta's brain.
