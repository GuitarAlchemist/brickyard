Delta answers sooner: a word at once when Claude has to think, simple questions answered on the spot, and ears that
can run on the graphics card with the processor as a fallback. Its window shows what it heard but let pass.

```
/plugin marketplace add GuitarAlchemist/brickyard
/plugin install delta@brickyard
/delta:setup
```

If Delta is already installed, run `/delta:setup` again to install this version: it keeps your language and the
copies it recorded.

## What changed

- **A word at once.** When a sentence goes to Claude, Delta says a short word right away, « Je regarde. » or « Une
  seconde. », in turn, at no cost, then Claude's answer. It says none while it speaks, and drops the word when the
  answer is already waiting or when it would come late.
- **Simple questions answered on the spot.** With the optional local triage, its model also answers a few questions
  of fact from a sheet the brain writes at that moment: the date and the time, Delta's version, where Whisper runs, the
  small model, and what Brickyard and IX are. Anything else, and anything that asks for an action, goes to Claude.
- **Ears with a fallback.** `"whisper_fallback"` in `delta.json` names a second whisper-server, such as the CPU build
  behind a build for your graphics card in `"whisper"`. Delta hears with the first that starts, and with the next when
  it cannot start, dies, or does not answer within 30 s, then hears the sentence again. On the author's machine a GPU
  build hears a sentence in about 0.2 s, against 2 s on the processor.
- **What Delta let pass shows on screen.** A sentence it ignored because it did not start with « Delta » shows for five
  seconds under the robot, with how to call it, so that you can tell a Delta that did not hear from one that was not
  called. Like the conversations on the shelf, it shows what was said in the room: mind it while the window is shared
  or streamed.
- **Hearts.** "Delta, I love you", or « je t'aime », sends red hearts rising from its chest while it smiles.
- **A window you closed stays closed**, even when another Delta started it, until you ask for it.
- **The other device is heard.** The local triage no longer drops what a device says through the gateway, since
  pressing its button addressed Delta. Each `/said` event now names the `utterance_id` it answers, and `by`: claude,
  local or ack.

Windows 10 or 11 x64 only, with a Vulkan 1.3 GPU and a microphone.
