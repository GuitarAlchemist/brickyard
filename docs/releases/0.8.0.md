Delta's hall opens onto the streets of Ottawa, its gallery shows the learn site's courses and demos, the office gives
each Claude Code session a robot in a round tower, and a Caster button shows Delta's window on a television through
any Chromecast.

```
/plugin marketplace add GuitarAlchemist/brickyard
/plugin install delta@brickyard
/delta:setup
```

If Delta is already installed, update the marketplace and the plugin, then run `/delta:setup` again to install this
version: it keeps your language and the copies it recorded.

```
claude plugin marketplace update brickyard
claude plugin update delta@brickyard
```

## What changed

- **The streets of Ottawa.** The hall's roll-up door opens onto the streets around it, from OpenStreetMap, with
  windows, roofs, a day's sky and the buildings' shadows; the buildings stop the walker. V rides a scooter, X flies a
  drone.
- **Google's imagery, on your own key.** With `GOOGLE_MAPS_API_KEY` set, Street View's panoramas of Google's own cars
  are shown at street level near where they were taken, with Google's attribution and its report-a-problem link. G
  fetches Google's Photorealistic 3D Tiles. Nothing is kept on disk. A ledger in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\delta` counts the
  requests and stops them at each month's free share for Street View, and at the free 1,000 plus 10 USD for the 3D
  tiles: they cost money past that, on your Google account.
- **The television.** The Caster button casts Delta's window to a Chromecast on the network, through the phone
  gateway (0.7.1's gateway does not cast: it needs this one). With several Chromecasts the window asks which.
- **The gallery.** The left wall shows the learn site's courses as books, its demos in water globes and its artifacts
  in glass paperweights; a lectern reads the courses. A click opens the page. The globes' pictures and videos, and the
  screen the nearest video is projected on, need them fetched first with `tools/gallery-pictures` from a checkout.
- **The office.** The robots stand in a round tower, a cubicle per wmux workspace, each with two screens. A waiting
  session shows a question mark over its robot, and its question can be answered from the scene. The rings turn by
  hand and coast.
- **The building.** A gymnasium wing, cast-concrete walls with their formwork, oak and brushed metal, and frosted
  windows. Gaia's planet turns by hand.
- **The knots' floor.** The deck's 21 sailors' knots in laid rope, each read by IX, when IX is installed.
- **RTX.** DLSS Super Resolution on the raster pass, under the same conditions as 0.7.1's DLSS.
- **F5** reloads the scene in full.

Still without the camera's hands.

Windows 10 or 11 x64 only, with a Vulkan 1.3 GPU and a microphone.
