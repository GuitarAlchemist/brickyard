```
/plugin marketplace add GuitarAlchemist/brickyard
/plugin install delta@brickyard
/delta:setup
```

version: it keeps your language and the copies it recorded.

```
claude plugin marketplace update brickyard
claude plugin update delta@brickyard
```

## What changed

- **Loose fibres.** Close to the tables, the rope shows the stray fibres a natural-fibre rope has, lying back along
  it, and a tuft at each cut strand.
- **Fibre light.** The strands' shaded sides are a warm brown, light passing into the fibres, and their highlight
  runs along the yarns.
- **Whipped ends.** The whipping stops just short of the rope's end, as wide as the rope, and the cut strands show
  past it.
- **Levels of detail.** Each knot is drawn finer near the eye and coarser away from it, its fibres only near the tables,
  and not at all when out of the picture: about a quarter of the rope's vertices on the tables' overall view.
- **Google's ledger.** A 3D tile request refused at the month's cap is not asked again.
