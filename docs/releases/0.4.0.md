Delta's window no longer closes by itself, comes back after a crash, and its shelf lists your past conversations.
Another device can speak to Delta through a gateway on this computer.

```
/plugin marketplace add GuitarAlchemist/brickyard
/plugin install delta@brickyard
/delta:setup
```

If Delta is already installed, run `/delta:setup` again to install this version: it keeps your language and the
copies it recorded.

## What changed

- **Two crashes in 0.3.1 are fixed.**
  - Resizing Delta's window quickly could close it: the graphics driver reports the old size while a frame is shown,
    and the scene now rebuilds its swapchain instead of stopping.
  - Opening a session from the shelf closed the window: its "Started" row asked .NET for a culture the scene does not
    load. Dates and numbers now come from Delta's own words, in English and French.
- **The window comes back.** If it crashes, the brain starts it again at the next thing Delta has to show, at most
  every 10 s. A window you closed stays closed until you ask Delta to move it or open its shelf. One that crashes
  within 30 s of its start is not restarted in a loop. The previous window's log is kept as `scene.prev.log`.
- **The shelf lists past conversations.** "Delta, show the conversations" (« montre les conversations ») lists this
  computer's Claude Code conversations by project, the most recent first, each opening onto its last requests. They
  show your own words in Delta's window: keep that section closed while the window is shared or streamed.
- **The optional local triage wakes up again.** When Ollama had unloaded its model, each sentence waited 4 s and then
  went to Claude. After a failure the model now loads in the background, and the next sentence finds it ready.
- **Another device can speak to Delta.** A gateway on this computer posts what the device heard to `POST /hear` and
  plays what Delta says from `GET /said`, on the hooks' address (127.0.0.1:8807). Both need the secret the brain writes
  at each start to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\delta\remote.secret`. A sentence from the device needs no « Delta »; a chime tells
  the room, and Claude asks you to confirm at the computer anything destructive it asks for. The whole address now
  refuses requests from web pages (an `Origin` header) or for another host than 127.0.0.1, localhost or ::1. No
  gateway ships with this release.

Windows 10 or 11 x64 only, with a Vulkan 1.3 GPU and a microphone.
